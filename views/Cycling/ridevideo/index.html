RideVideo

Put your ride data on the video

Add your action-camera clips and the Strava GPX of the same ride. The app syncs them, draws heart rate, speed, elevation and the route on top, and makes a highlight reel or the full ride.

Or drop the files here. Everything stays on this device; nothing is uploaded.

  • Strava: open the activity → ⋯ → Export GPX
  • Keep the camera's date/time stamp on: the app reads it to sync
  • Several clips from one ride are fine; select them all
