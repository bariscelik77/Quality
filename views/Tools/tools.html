Sampling plan – ISO 2859-1 / ANSI Z1.4

How many items to inspect from a lot, and how many defectives you can accept. Single sampling, normal inspection, nonconforming items (AQL up to 10%).

General level II is the default unless the specification says otherwise.

Based on ISO 2859-1 Table 1 (code letters) and Table 2-A (single sampling, normal inspection). For contractual use, confirm against the standard.

Weld repair rate

Repair rate from radiography/UT results, by joints or by length, checked against your project's acceptance target.

Amber when within this margin below target.

Repair rate = rejected ÷ tested × 100. Use the definition in your project quality plan (welder-, process- or line-based) when reporting.

Punch burn-down forecast

When will the open punch list reach zero? Uses your weekly closure and new-item rates.

Take the average of the last 4 weeks for both rates.
