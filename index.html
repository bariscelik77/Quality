About

Quality from first weld to start-up

I lead the Employer QA/QC team at NEOM Green Hydrogen Company across the HIDC, GHE and PWE (Grid, Wind, Solar) packages: reviewing contractors' quality plans, auditing their quality programmes and reporting performance through to handover.

Before NEOM I ran quality and completions for owners and EPC contractors on refineries, LNG, petrochemical, upstream and power projects for BP, Saudi Aramco, ADNOC and LUKOIL. I build project quality systems to ISO 9001 and client standards, and I like turning completion data into dashboards people actually use.

I work in English and Turkish.
